La Matriz Consulting

A mirror, not a verdict. Turn your felt, sensory data into projected trajectories you can act on.

The Main Workflow: Your Data-Somatic Journey

Every decision is an act of sorting. Traditional frameworks for problem solving leave out most of what you actually feel, dismissing gut and body signals as noise. The La Matriz workflow puts your vague data back into the calculation through a private, local, interactive mirror.

Step 1: The Pluriverse Journal

Hold it & Name it. We begin with reflective text prompts designed for slow, deliberate dialogue. You enter your current state—your challenges, stuck points, and nuances—in your vague language. The tool plots your data into a framework with a visible color trajectory indicating voltage (energy transitions) and essential rest ratios. Send me the Pluriverse Letter and your export of your raw JSON data.

Step 2: The Color Plotter

Calibrate & Act. From your own words, I map pre-determined and personalized sequences in a custom PDF. Your narrative maps directly onto La Matrice—a 15-color semantic-somatic framework. You read the sequences, choose what coheres, and author your own next steps across mind, body, and speech.
